# Social Brain Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** June 2025  
**Last Updated:** June 2025

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to Social Brain ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices when you use our Social Brain iOS application (the "App").

Social Brain is a Personal Relationship Management (PRM) app designed to help users enhance the quality of their social interactions and nurture long-term relationships. **Important: Our app does not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal information on our servers.**

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

### 2.1 No Data Collection
Social Brain operates on a **local-only basis**. We do not:
- Collect any personal information from you
- Transmit your data to our servers
- Store your information in the cloud (except optional iCloud sync)
- Track your usage or behavior
- Share your data with third parties
- Use analytics or tracking services

### 2.2 What We Don't Have Access To
- Your contact information
- Your social notes and interactions
- Your relationship data
- Your conversation topics
- Your personal preferences
- Any other information you enter in the app

## 3. How Your Data is Stored

### 3.1 Local Storage Only
All your data is stored **locally on your device** using iOS Core Data:
- Your data never leaves your device
- We have no access to your information
- Your data is encrypted using iOS security features
- You have complete control over your data

### 3.2 Optional iCloud Sync
If you choose to enable iCloud sync:
- Your data syncs across your devices using Apple's iCloud service
- This is handled entirely by Apple, not by us
- iCloud sync is subject to Apple's privacy policy and terms of service
- You can disable iCloud sync at any time in your device settings

## 4. Data Security

### 4.1 Your Device Security
- All data is encrypted using iOS encryption standards
- Your data is protected by your device's security features
- We cannot access your data even if we wanted to
- Your privacy is protected by design

### 4.2 No Server Infrastructure
- We don't operate any servers to store your data
- We don't have databases containing user information
- We don't process your data on our systems
- Your data stays on your device at all times

## 5. Third-Party Services

### 5.1 No Third-Party Data Sharing
We do not share your data with any third parties because:
- We don't collect your data in the first place
- We don't have access to your information
- We don't use third-party analytics or tracking services

### 5.2 Apple Services Only
The only external service we interact with is:
- **iCloud** (optional): For syncing your data across devices, handled entirely by Apple

## 6. Your Rights and Control

Since we don't collect your data, you have complete control:

- **Full Ownership:** All your data belongs to you
- **Local Control:** You can access, modify, or delete your data anytime
- **No Account Required:** No registration or account creation needed
- **Complete Privacy:** Your data never leaves your device unless you choose iCloud sync

## 7. Children's Privacy

Social Brain is not intended for children under 13 years of age. Since we don't collect any data, there's no risk of collecting children's information.

## 8. International Data Transfers

Since we don't collect or store your data, there are no international data transfers. Your data remains on your device and, if you choose, on Apple's iCloud service.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:
- Posting the new Privacy Policy in the app
- Sending you a notification through the app
- Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy

Your continued use of the app after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

## 11. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

Since we don't collect personal information, CCPA rights don't apply to our data practices. However, you have complete control over your locally stored data.

## 12. European Privacy Rights (GDPR)

Since we don't collect or process personal data, GDPR rights don't apply to our data practices. Your data remains under your complete control on your device.

## 13. Why This Approach?

We believe in **privacy by design**. By not collecting any user data:
- Your privacy is protected by default
- There's no risk of data breaches
- You maintain complete control
- The app works entirely offline
- No tracking or surveillance
